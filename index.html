About

I am a final-year B.Eng. student in Robotics and Automation Engineering at the Institute of Field Robotics (FIBO), King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi, with a GPAX of 3.91 / 4.00. My work spans two connected areas: robot learning and autonomous mobile robots.

In robot learning, I work with deep reinforcement learning for legged robots, including continual learning, imitation and inverse reinforcement learning, and sim-to-real transfer to real quadrupeds. In mobile robotics, I have built LiDAR-based autonomous systems, from self-driving and racing vehicles to an EGAT-funded robot for cleaning floating solar panels, which was published at IEEE RAAI 2025. I am currently a research intern at the BRAIN Lab, VISTEC.

I enjoy working across the full robot stack, from embedded control and sensors to simulation and learning-based control, and I value seeing ideas work on real hardware. Outside research, I have been a teaching assistant at FIBO since 2024 and have led academic programmes for robotics camps, which have taught me to explain technical ideas clearly and work well in teams.

AMP-Anchored Continual RL

Apr – May 2026

Course Research Project, Deep Reinforcement Learning · GitHub

Can a single policy on a Unitree Go2 learn three incompatible gaits in sequence (Trot, then Canter, then Turn) without forgetting earlier ones, while keeping the motion natural?

  • Extended Learning without Forgetting to continuous actions as a KL penalty towards frozen teacher policies, with a weight that adapts to how well earlier gaits are retained.
  • Added Adversarial Motion Priors (AMP), a discriminator trained on Go2 motion clips used as a style reward, to anchor behaviour to reference motions and stop unnatural shortcuts such as sliding. Compared universal, per-task and continuously trained discriminators with fixed and adaptive reward blending.
  • Trained with PPO in Isaac Lab using 4,096 parallel environments.
  • Without retention, earlier gaits dropped by 32–40%. With LwF + AMP, Trot reached 0.824, above the Trot-only specialist (0.746), and the newest task reached 0.679.
  • Continual Learning
